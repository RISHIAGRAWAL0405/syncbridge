The Vision

A Decade of
Cinematic Artistry

Founded in 2015 by award-winning photographer Alexandre Moreau, Lumière Studio was born from a singular belief: that photography is not merely documentation — it is the art of freezing emotion, light, and truth into a single, eternal frame.

From intimate weddings in Tuscany to high-fashion campaigns in Tokyo, our work spans continents and cultures, united by an unwavering commitment to excellence and a deeply personal approach to every story we tell.
